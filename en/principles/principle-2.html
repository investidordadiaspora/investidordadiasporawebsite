Contribute to Community Development

Strengthening ties with the local community through social responsibility practices and positive impact

Overview

Companies play a vital role in the locations where they are established, and the importance of incorporating values that contribute to the local community cannot be underestimated. Beyond seeking profits, companies have a responsibility to contribute to the well-being of these areas. By adopting practices that promote sustainable development, social responsibility, and positive impact, companies will strengthen their ties with the community.

Essential Commitment

This principle reflects the commitment to business practices that promote community well-being and long-term social sustainability.

Recommended Measures

1

Community Partnerships

Establish collaborations with local institutions to maximize social impact.

  • Partnerships with charities and community entities
2

Corporate Volunteering

Involve employees in actions that directly benefit the community.

  • Volunteering initiatives that involve company employees in community activities
3

Educational Development

Promote training and qualification for the local labor market.

  • Educational initiatives to promote skills necessary for the labor market
4

Cultural Preservation

Support the maintenance and promotion of the region's cultural identity.

  • Support for programs that preserve and promote the region's culture
5

Infrastructure Improvements

Contribute to the enhancement of essential public facilities.

  • Contributions to improvements in schools, hospitals, or other public facilities
6

Local Products and Services

Develop offers adapted to the specific needs of the community.

  • Development of products or services that meet the specific needs of the community
