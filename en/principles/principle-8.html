Social Protection

Ensuring the well-being and security of our employees through robust social policies

Overview

Social protection in the workplace refers to the policies and programs designed to reduce poverty and vulnerability by promoting efficient labor markets, diminishing people's exposure to risks, and enhancing their capacity to manage economic and social risks, such as unemployment, exclusion, sickness, disability, and old age. Robust social protection systems ensure that employees have a safety net, fostering a more secure, productive, and loyal workforce.

Essential Commitment

This principle reflects the commitment to business practices that prioritize the social security and well-being of all employees.

Recommended Measures

1

Social Security Contributions

Ensure all employees are correctly registered and contributions are paid.

  • Adhere to all legal requirements for social security contributions for all employees
2

Health and Safety Insurance

Provide comprehensive insurance for workplace accidents and health.

  • Provide adequate health and workplace accident insurance to ensure employee protection
3

Retirement Planning

Support employees in planning for their long-term financial security.

  • Offer or facilitate access to retirement savings plans and pension schemes
4

Leave Policies

Establish fair and supportive leave policies for various life events.

  • Implement supportive policies for sick leave, maternity/paternity leave, and other essential life events
